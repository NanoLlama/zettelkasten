Handout: Retrieval practice study (sample) 
This is a sample handout used to demonstrate that text inside attached PDFs is
searchable. 
Conditions 
1. Study, study, study, study (rereading only)
2. Study, test, test, test (retrieval practice)
After one week, the retrieval group recalled substantially more. One proposed
mechanism is that effortful recall strengthens consolidation in the hippocampus
and makes memories easier to reach later.
