Instead of choosing a topic and then researching it, keep writing permanent notes about whatever interests you. Over time, some areas become dense with links: those clusters are your topics.

Choosing a topic this way means you start writing with material already in hand: [[202609101030|Drafting from notes avoids the blank page]].
